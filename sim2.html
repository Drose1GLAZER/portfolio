TL;DR // NO READING REQUIRED

Wait—there’s no traditional “plot.” Here’s how the book actually works.

The Anthropocene Reviewed isn't a novel with chapters that follow one story. It is a collection of 44 personal essays where author John Green reviews random parts of our human-centered planet (called the Anthropocene) on a 5-star scale—from Halley’s Comet to Diet Dr Pepper.

John's Secret Formula 3 ACTS / ESSAY
01 // WEIRD HISTORY

Starts with fascinating facts about a random topic (e.g., how Monopoly was stolen).

02 // PERSONAL PIVOT

Connects that topic to his own life—his OCD, anxiety, family, or loneliness.

03 // 5-STAR VERDICT

Concludes with a deeper truth about being human and assigns a rating out of 5 stars.
